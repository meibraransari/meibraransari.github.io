01.About Me

Hi, I'm Ibrar, an experienced Lead System Administrator and DevOps Engineer with over 9 years of hands-on experience in information technology.

I specialize in cloud infrastructure architecture (AWS & DigitalOcean), containerization (Docker), continuous integration & delivery (CI/CD), virtualization (Proxmox and VMware ESXi), web hosting server management (WHM/cPanel/Plesk), and enterprise endpoint management (Ivanti UEM suite).

Throughout my career across leading IT organizations in India, the US, and the UAE, I have successfully delivered high-availability solutions, implemented infrastructure monitoring with Zabbix and Grafana, deployed containerized microservices using Docker, and integrated third-party enterprise applications.
